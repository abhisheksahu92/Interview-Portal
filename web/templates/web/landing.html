Interview Portal gives every company its own workspace: configurable pipeline stages, a shared kanban board, structured interview reviews, and AI screening that summarises each résumé against the role and scores fit before anyone opens a CV.

Multi-tenant by design

Each company gets isolated jobs, candidates, skills and question banks. Owners invite recruiters and interviewers with scoped roles.

Configurable pipelines

Screening, assessment, L1/L2, HR, offer — rename, reorder or drop stages per job, and mark the ones that require an online assessment.

AI-assisted screening

Generate skill-tagged question banks from a job description, grade free-text answers, and get a fit score per application — degrading gracefully when no AI key is configured.

How it works

  1. Create your company workspace and post a job — a default pipeline is seeded for you.
  2. Candidates apply from the public openings board with a reusable profile and résumé.
  3. Recruiters triage on the kanban board; interviewers work a review queue.
  4. Assessments and AI fit scores keep the top of the funnel manageable.
