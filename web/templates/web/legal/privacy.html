This notice explains what personal data {{ business_name }} collects, why, who it goes to, how long we keep it, and what you can ask us to do about it. It is written to meet the itemised-notice requirement of India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”).

1. Two different roles

For workspace users — owners, recruiters and interviewers — we are the Data Fiduciary: we decide why and how your account data is processed, and this notice is our notice to you.

For candidate data uploaded by a company — résumés, contact details, interview feedback, assessment answers, video recordings — the company that runs the workspace is the Data Fiduciary and we are its Data Processor. If you are a candidate and want your data changed or removed from a particular company’s workspace, we will pass your request to that company; we act on their instruction, not our own judgement. Where you created your own candidate account and profile with us directly, we are the Fiduciary for that profile.

4. Transfers outside India

Some of these processors — the model provider, the email provider, Sentry, Stripe — operate outside India, so data described above may be processed abroad. The application database and uploaded files are hosted in the region the deployment is configured for.

5. How long we keep it

  • Workspace and candidate records — for as long as the workspace is active, because a hiring history is the point of the product. When a workspace is closed we delete or return its data on request.
  • Candidate accounts — until the candidate asks us to delete them.
  • Invoices, GST records and payment references — retained for the period Indian tax and company law requires, even after an account closes.
  • Error traces and technical logs — short-lived, per the retention of the logging and error tools.

We are being straight with you: there is no automated purge job today. Deletion happens when it is requested, by hand.

6. Your rights under the DPDP Act

You may ask us to:

  • Access — a summary of the personal data we process about you and who it has been shared with;
  • Correct or complete — fix data that is wrong, incomplete or out of date;
  • Erase — delete data we no longer need for the purpose it was collected for, or where you withdraw consent (subject to the retention we are legally required to keep);
  • Withdraw consent — as easily as it was given; this does not undo processing already carried out;
  • Nominate — name someone to exercise these rights if you die or become incapacitated;
  • Complain — to our Grievance Officer, and after that to the Data Protection Board of India.
How to make a request: email {{ contact_email }} from the address on your account. There is no self-service export or delete button in the product yet — we are not claiming one exists. Requests are handled manually and we aim to respond within 30 days. If your data sits in a company’s workspace, we will route the request to that company, since they decide what happens to it.

8. Children

The Service is for working adults. We do not knowingly process the data of anyone under 18. If you believe a child’s data is here, write to us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Traffic is served over HTTPS, passwords are stored as salted hashes, every query is scoped to the workspace it belongs to, and candidate-facing links (offers, booking pages, BGV consent, client portals) are unguessable signed tokens that expire. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data we will notify you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

10. Changes

We will update this notice as the product changes. The version and date at the top show the current text, and we record which version each user accepted at signup.
