5. AI screening — what it is and is not

The Service uses a third-party large language model to generate screening questions from a job description, grade free-text answers, summarise a résumé against a role with a fit score, extract structured fields from bulk résumé imports, and summarise recorded video answers. To do this, the relevant text — résumé text, the job description, the candidate’s answers or transcript — is sent to our model provider (currently Anthropic, or Google Gemini where that key is configured instead).

These outputs are assistive and probabilistic. They are not a decision, they can be wrong, and they must not be the sole basis on which a candidate is rejected. A human must review before a rejection, an offer, or any other decision with a legal or similarly significant effect. We give no warranty about the accuracy, fairness or fitness of any AI output. If no model key is configured, AI features simply do nothing — the rest of the Service still works.

6. Background verification

Background checks are resold: you order a check package, the candidate is sent a consent link, and only after the candidate consents is the order placed with the verification provider and your account charged. If the candidate does not consent, no check is run and nothing is charged. You must not use a verification report for any purpose other than assessing that candidate for that role.
