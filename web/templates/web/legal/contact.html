Grievance Officer

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can raise a complaint about how your personal data is handled with our Grievance Officer: {% if grievance_officer %} {{ grievance_officer }}, {% else %} name to be filled in, {% endif %} {{ grievance_email }}. We aim to acknowledge within 7 days and resolve within 30. If you are not satisfied, you may escalate to the Data Protection Board of India.

What to write to us about

  • Billing, invoices, GST — quote the invoice number.
  • Cancellation — from the workspace owner’s email address; see the Refund & Cancellation Policy.
  • Access, correction or deletion of your data — from the email address on your account; see the Privacy Policy. These are handled manually, so please be specific about what you want removed.
  • A candidate profile in a company’s workspace — tell us which company; they decide what happens to it and we will route your request to them.
  • Security issues — report them privately to {{ contact_email }} rather than in public.

Support is by email. We aim to reply on working days, Monday to Friday.
